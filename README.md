# Quincaillerie Manager

Windows desktop application for managing a hardware shop, built with Tauri, React, TypeScript, and SQLite.

## Development

- `npm run dev` starts the frontend.
- `npm run tauri dev` starts the desktop application with its local SQLite database.
- `npm run build` checks TypeScript and creates the frontend production build.
- `cargo test --manifest-path src-tauri/Cargo.toml` runs the Rust/database tests.

Business setup, units, categories, and brands are available from **Settings** in the desktop application. Product creation and the searchable, paginated catalogue are available under **Products**, where category and brand are optional. Products can also have additional selling units with conversion factors to their base unit. The **Products** and **Inventory** pages allow the base-unit selling price to be changed and record each price period; a new regular price cannot be below the highest purchase cost of stock on hand. Inventory shows on-hand quantities, purchase-layer stock value, selling price, and stock alerts without displaying average cost. Stock value and cost of goods sold use FIFO purchase-cost layers, so each actual receipt cost is retained; the migration reconstructs layers from stock movements and uses the existing average cost only for any unmatched legacy quantity. A sale below the actual FIFO cost can be authorized with a required reason, which is recorded on the sale. The **Purchases** page records supplier invoices and product quantities/costs, warns on duplicate supplier invoice numbers and requires explicit confirmation to record a duplicate, supports partial deliveries, and records partial or full invoice payments with a payment method and history. Each invoice shows the amount paid and remaining balance; recording a payment does not change delivery status or stock. Stock increases only for quantities explicitly received against an invoice; receipts retain delivery references and update inventory costs and movement history atomically. Discounts, taxes, extra transport costs, and attachments are not implemented yet. The optional selling-price update remains available on direct inventory receiving and is checked against the new purchase cost. The **Point of Sale** supports business-scoped product search, a base-unit cart, paid or credit sales, customer selection, split payments, stock deduction, and an in-app receipt. Sales and their stock movements are written atomically, checkout records the responsible user, and receipt lines retain the listed and charged price; a changed per-sale price requires a reason and does not change the product's regular price. The **Sales History** page provides business-scoped search by receipt, customer, product, or SKU, date filters, pagination, payment totals, and transaction details based on the saved sale-line and payment records. Returns, sale editing, and printable receipts are not implemented yet. The **Suppliers** page supports business-scoped supplier contact, address, and tax details, with editing and inactive status. Supplier payment balances and history are available on each purchase invoice; aggregate supplier account statements and invoice attachments are not implemented yet. Movement user attribution remains empty until login is implemented. Business records are stored in the local database; the frontend-only Vite preview cannot access these Tauri commands. Product creation and purchase/receipt unit costs are entered in whole RWF; saved money uses the database's hundredths-of-RWF scale, and selling-price edits support cents.
